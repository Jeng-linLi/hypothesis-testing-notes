Q1§01–02 · 全課地圖與反證法二項分佈
題目　延續講義開場的品管情境：長期不良率標準是 p = 0.05，抽 n = 100 件。這次抽到 8 件不良（不是講義裡的 10 件）。
(a) 計算 P(X ≥ 8)。(b) 在 α = .05 下判決。(c) 若抽到 4 件，P(X ≥ 4) = 0.742，能不能說「製程合格」？
Question. Continue the opening quality-control example: the long-run defect standard is p = 0.05, and n = 100 items are sampled. This time 8 defectives are found (not the 10 in the notes). (a) Compute P(X ≥ 8). (b) Decide at α = .05. (c) If only 4 were found, with P(X ≥ 4) = 0.742, can we declare the process acceptable?
顯示詳解 / Show solution
  1. 什麼是假設檢定 / What a test is
    反證法＋機率：先假設「製程沒問題」，再問「如果真的沒問題，抽到這麼糟的樣本有多罕見？」罕見到不合理就推翻假設。
    Proof by contradiction plus probability: assume "the process is fine", then ask how unlikely a sample this bad would be. If it is implausibly rare, reject the assumption.
  2. 算尾巴面積 / Compute the tail area
    X ~ Binomial(100, 0.05)。
    P(X ≥ 8) = 1 − Σ(k=0..7) C(100,k)(0.05)^k(0.95)^(100−k) = 0.1280。
    （講義的 10 件是 0.0282，6 件是 0.384，4 件是 0.742。）
    X ~ Binomial(100, 0.05).
    P(X ≥ 8) = 1 − Σ(k=0..7) C(100,k)(0.05)^k(0.95)^(100−k) = 0.1280.
    (The notes give 0.0282 for 10, 0.384 for 6, and 0.742 for 4.)
  3. 判決 / Decision
    0.1280 > 0.05 → 不拒絕。8 件不良雖然高於期望的 5 件，但這種波動並不罕見（約 1/8 的機會），不足以認定不良率超標。
    0.1280 > 0.05 → do not reject. Eight defectives exceed the expected five, but such fluctuation is not rare (about 1 in 8), so it is not enough to claim a higher defect rate.
  4. 第 (c) 問的陷阱 / The trap in (c)
    0.742 很大，只能說「沒抓到把柄」。「不拒絕」≠「證明合格」——就像法庭判無罪不等於他真的沒做。這是 §02 反覆強調、也是最常考的一句話。
    0.742 is large, so we simply "did not catch anything". "Fail to reject" ≠ "proved acceptable"—like a not-guilty verdict. This is the single most-tested sentence in §02.
答案：(a) 0.1280；(b) 不拒絕；(c) 不能，欠缺證據不等於證明合格。Answer: (a) 0.1280; (b) do not reject; (c) no—lack of evidence is not proof of acceptance.
Q2§03 · 虛無與對立假設寫 H₀/Ha
題目　設 μ 為某母體平均值。把下列五句話分別寫成 H₀ 與 Ha，並標出尾巴型態：
(a) 平均值「至少 50」；(b) 平均值「低於 50」；(c) 平均值「等於 50」；(d) 平均值「偏離 50」；(e) 平均值「不超過 50」但我們想證明它超標。
Question. Let μ be a population mean. Write H₀ and Ha for each statement and label the tail: (a) the mean is "at least 50"; (b) "below 50"; (c) "equal to 50"; (d) "different from 50"; (e) "at most 50" but we want to show it exceeds 50.
顯示詳解 / Show solution
  1. 唯一的規則 / The one rule
    想證明的事 → Ha；現況或含等號的保守說法 → H₀。等號永遠在 H₀；Ha 決定了尾巴方向。
    What you want to establish → Ha; the status quo (which contains equality) → H₀. Equality always sits in H₀, and Ha determines the tail.
  2. 逐題 / One by one
    (a) 這是現況（至少 50），若要檢驗它是否被違反：H₀: μ ≥ 50 vs Ha: μ < 50，左尾。
    (b) 想證明低於：H₀: μ ≥ 50 vs Ha: μ < 50，左尾。
    (c) H₀: μ = 50 vs Ha: μ ≠ 50，雙尾。
    (d) 同 (c)，「偏離」就是不等於，雙尾。
    (e) H₀: μ ≤ 50 vs Ha: μ > 50，右尾（此處要證明的是超標，不是原說法本身）。
    (a) This is the status quo; to test whether it is violated: H₀: μ ≥ 50 vs Ha: μ < 50, lower tail.
    (b) Want to show below: H₀: μ ≥ 50 vs Ha: μ < 50, lower tail.
    (c) H₀: μ = 50 vs Ha: μ ≠ 50, two-tailed.
    (d) Same as (c): "different" means ≠, two-tailed.
    (e) H₀: μ ≤ 50 vs Ha: μ > 50, upper tail (here we want to establish the excess).
  3. (a) 與 (b) 一樣？/ Why (a) and (b) coincide
    對。一段話陳述現況，與想推翻它，會得到同一組假設——因為等號永遠留給 H₀。差別只在研究動機，不在數學形式。
    Yes. Stating a status quo and wanting to overturn it produce the same pair, because equality always stays in H₀. The difference is motivation, not mathematics.
答案：(a)(b) 左尾 Ha: μ<50；(c)(d) 雙尾 Ha: μ≠50；(e) 右尾 Ha: μ>50。Answer: (a)(b) lower, Ha: μ<50; (c)(d) two-tailed, Ha: μ≠50; (e) upper, Ha: μ>50.
Q3§04 · 單尾與雙尾尾巴的代價
題目　同一筆資料算出 z = 1.90（σ 已知的右側檢定）。
(a) 若 Ha: μ > μ₀、α = .05，拒絕嗎？
(b) 若 Ha: μ ≠ μ₀、α = .05，拒絕嗎？
(c) 有人說「雙尾沒顯著，那改 reporting 單尾就好了」。錯在哪？
Question. One dataset gives z = 1.90 (σ known, upper side). (a) With Ha: μ > μ₀, α = .05, reject? (b) With Ha: μ ≠ μ₀, α = .05, reject? (c) Someone says "if two-sided is not significant, just report one-sided". What is wrong with that?
顯示詳解 / Show solution
  1. 單尾判決 / One-tailed decision
    右尾 z₀.₀₅ = 1.645。1.90 > 1.645 → 拒絕。p = P(Z > 1.90) = 0.0287 < .05。
    Upper tail z₀.₀₅ = 1.645. Since 1.90 > 1.645 → reject. p = P(Z > 1.90) = 0.0287 < .05.
  2. 雙尾判決 / Two-tailed decision
    雙尾要把 α 拆兩邊，z₀.₀₂₅ = 1.960。1.90 < 1.960 → 不拒絕。p = 2 × 0.0287 = 0.0574 > .05。
    Two-tailed splits α: z₀.₀₂₅ = 1.960. Since 1.90 < 1.960 → do not reject. p = 2 × 0.0287 = 0.0574 > .05.
  3. 同一筆資料，兩個結論 / Same data, two verdicts
    這不是矛盾，是代價不同：單尾把全部 5% 放在一邊，所以比較容易拒絕；雙尾分兩邊，每次都要跨過 1.960 的高門檻。power 的增益是用「完全放棄另一個方向」換來的。
    Not a contradiction—a different price. One-tailed puts all 5% on one side and is easier to reject; two-tailed splits it and needs the higher 1.960 bar. The power gain is bought by giving up the other direction entirely.
  4. 為什麼不能事後改 / Why you cannot switch later
    看到 z 是正的就選右尾，等於利用資料決定假設，真正的型一錯誤率會變成約 10%（兩邊各有 5% 被拿去用）。方向必須在收資料前定好——這正是 §15 十步流程把「定義研究問題」放在第 1 步的原因。
    Choosing the upper tail after seeing a positive z lets the data pick the hypothesis; the true Type I rate becomes about 10% (5% from each side). The direction must be fixed before collecting data—exactly why §15 puts "define the research question" at step 1.
答案：(a) 拒絕（p=0.0287）；(b) 不拒絕（p=0.0574）；(c) 事後選尾巴是 p-hacking，會讓 α 失控。Answer: (a) reject (p=0.0287); (b) do not reject (p=0.0574); (c) choosing a tail after seeing data is p-hacking and inflates α.
Q4§05 · 型一與型二錯誤期望誤判
題目　一個檢定長期重複使用。研究人員做的 100 次檢定中，有 60 次 H₀ 其實為真、40 次 Ha 其實為真。已知 α = .05、β = .20。
(a) 期望犯幾次型一錯誤？(b) 期望犯幾次型二錯誤？(c) 期望總共幾次判決是錯的？(d) 若把 α 降到 .01（β 升到 .35），總錯誤次數變成多少？
Question. A test is used repeatedly. Of 100 applications, H₀ is actually true in 60 and Ha is actually true in 40. Given α = .05 and β = .20: (a) expected Type I errors? (b) expected Type II errors? (c) total expected wrong decisions? (d) If α drops to .01 and β rises to .35, what is the new total?
顯示詳解 / Show solution
  1. 型一只發生在 H₀ 為真時 / Type I only when H₀ is true
    60 × 0.05 = 3 次。型一是「沒問題卻誤報」。
    60 × 0.05 = 3. Type I is a false alarm when nothing is wrong.
  2. 型二只發生在 Ha 為真時 / Type II only when Ha is true
    40 × 0.20 = 8 次。型二是「有問題卻漏接」。
    40 × 0.20 = 8. Type II is a missed signal when there is something to find.
  3. 加總 / Total
    3 + 8 = 11 次，也就是 100 次裡約 11% 判錯。
    3 + 8 = 11, about 11% wrong decisions.
  4. 降低 α 之後 / After lowering α
    型一 60 × .01 = 0.6；型二 40 × .35 = 14；總共 14.6 次。錯誤反而變多——因為這批檢定裡「真的有效果」的比例不低，漏接的代價超過誤報省下的。
    這告訴我們：α 沒有「愈小愈好」的絕對標準，要看兩種錯誤的相對成本。想同時降低兩者只能加大 n。
    Type I 60 × .01 = 0.6; Type II 40 × .35 = 14; total 14.6. Errors increased, because real effects are common here and missed signals cost more than the false alarms saved.
    Lesson: smaller α is not automatically better—it depends on the relative cost of the two errors. Only a larger n lowers both.
答案：(a) 3 次；(b) 8 次；(c) 11 次；(d) 0.6 + 14 = 14.6 次，比原本更多。Answer: (a) 3; (b) 8; (c) 11; (d) 0.6 + 14 = 14.6, more than before.
Q5§06 · α、β 與取捨p-hacking
題目　某雙尾檢定得到 z = 1.83。
(a) 在 α = .05 判決；(b) 在 α = .10 判決；(c) 算出 p 值；(d) 研究助理說「反正 p = .067 很接近 .05，我們就寫 α = .10 並說顯著」。這在統計上有什麼問題？
Question. A two-tailed test gives z = 1.83. Decide at (a) α = .05 and (b) α = .10. (c) Find the p-value. (d) An assistant says "p = .067 is close to .05, so let's just declare α = .10 and call it significant". What is statistically wrong?
顯示詳解 / Show solution
  1. 兩種門檻 / Two thresholds
    α=.05：z₀.₀₂₅=1.960，1.83<1.960 → 不拒絕。
    α=.10：z₀.₀₅=1.645，1.83>1.645 → 拒絕。
    α=.05: z₀.₀₂₅=1.960, 1.83<1.960 → do not reject.
    α=.10: z₀.₀₅=1.645, 1.83>1.645 → reject.
  2. p 值 / p-value
    p = 2 × P(Z > 1.83) = 2 × 0.0336 = 0.0672。跟兩個門檻比對，結論完全一致。
    p = 2 × P(Z > 1.83) = 2 × 0.0336 = 0.0672. Both decisions agree with the p-value comparison.
  3. 為什麼不能事後換 α / Why α cannot be changed later
    α 是事先願意接受的誤報率，是規則不是結果。看到 p 值才挑門檻，等於偷偷放大真正的型一錯誤率——這就是 p-hacking 的技術本質。課件 §06 用這題當標準示範。
    α is the false-alarm rate you pre-commit to; it is a rule, not an outcome. Picking a threshold after seeing p secretly inflates the true Type I rate—this is p-hacking in technical form. The notes use this exact case as the canonical example in §06.
  4. 四個槓桿複習 / The four levers
    α↓ → β↑；n↑ → β↓；|μₐ−μ₀|↑ → β↓；σ↑ → β↑。想同時壓低 α 和 β，只有加大 n 或降低 σ。
    α↓ → β↑; n↑ → β↓; |μₐ−μ₀|↑ → β↓; σ↑ → β↑. To lower both α and β, increase n or reduce σ.
答案：p = 0.0672；α=.05 不拒絕，α=.10 拒絕；事後挑 α 是 p-hacking，α 必須事先固定。Answer: p = 0.0672; not rejected at .05, rejected at .10; switching α afterwards is p-hacking—fix α in advance.
Q6§07 · 臨界值法Z 右尾
題目　已知 σ = 12，H₀: μ = 80 vs Ha: μ > 80，n = 36、x̄ = 84.5、α = .01。用臨界值法完整判決，並把門檻換算到 x̄ 尺度。
Question. Given σ = 12, H₀: μ = 80 vs Ha: μ > 80, n = 36, x̄ = 84.5, α = .01. Decide using the critical-value method and convert the threshold to the x̄ scale.
顯示詳解 / Show solution
  1. 標準誤 / Standard error
    SE = 12/√36 = 2。
    SE = 12/√36 = 2.
  2. 算 z / Compute z
    z = (84.5 − 80)/2 = 2.25。
    z = (84.5 − 80)/2 = 2.25.
  3. 找臨界值 / Find the critical value
    右尾 α=.01 → z₀.₀₁ = 2.326。2.25 < 2.326 → 不拒絕。
    換到 x̄ 尺度：c = 80 + 2.326 × 2 = 84.6527；84.5 < 84.65，結論一致。
    Upper tail α=.01 → z₀.₀₁ = 2.326. Since 2.25 < 2.326 → do not reject.
    On the x̄ scale: c = 80 + 2.326 × 2 = 84.6527; 84.5 < 84.65, same verdict.
  4. 這題的直覺陷阱 / Intuition trap
    84.5 比 80 高了 4.5，看起來很多，但 α=.01 是很嚴的標準，需要 z > 2.326。這題在提醒：「差很多」和「統計顯著」是兩件事，門檻由 α 和 SE 共同決定。
    84.5 is 4.5 above 80, which looks like a lot—but α=.01 is strict and needs z > 2.326. "Big difference" and "statistically significant" are different claims; the cutoff is set by α and SE together.
答案：z = 2.25 < 2.326 → 不拒絕；c = 84.6527，x̄ = 84.5 < c。Answer: z = 2.25 < 2.326 → do not reject; c = 84.6527 and x̄ = 84.5 < c.
Q7§08 · P 值法與臨界值法等價
題目　用 Q6 的同一筆資料（z = 2.25，右尾，α = .01）。
(a) 用 p 值法判決；(b) 若改成雙尾 α = .05，結論會不會變？(c) 說明為什麼兩種方法（與 Q6 的臨界值法）永遠給出同一個答案。
Question. Use Q6's data (z = 2.25, upper tail, α = .01). (a) Decide with the p-value method. (b) Would the verdict change for a two-tailed test at α = .05? (c) Explain why this method and Q6's critical-value method always agree.
顯示詳解 / Show solution
  1. 右尾 p 值 / Upper-tail p
    p = P(Z > 2.25) = 0.01222。0.01222 > 0.01 → 不拒絕，與 Q6 完全一致。
    p = P(Z > 2.25) = 0.01222. Since 0.01222 > 0.01 → do not reject, exactly as in Q6.
  2. 改雙尾 α=.05 / Two-tailed at .05
    p = 2 × 0.01222 = 0.0244 < .05 → 拒絕。
    同一個 z，只因為 α 從 .01（單尾）改成 .05（雙尾）就翻盤——不是資料變了，是規則變了。
    p = 2 × 0.01222 = 0.0244 < .05 → reject.
    Same z, opposite verdict—only because the rule changed from .01 one-tailed to .05 two-tailed.
  3. 為什麼等價 / Why they are equivalent
    臨界值法是先設誤報率 α，再算出門檻 c；p 值法是先算出觀察值有多極端，再跟 α 比。兩者用的是同一條常態曲線、同一個尾巴面積，只是敘述方向相反：z > z_α ⟺ p < α。
    The critical-value method fixes α then finds the cutoff c; the p-value method measures how extreme the observation is then compares with α. They use the same curve and the same tail area, just phrased in opposite directions: z > z_α ⟺ p < α.
  4. 何時 p 值較方便 / When p is handier
    p 值讓讀者自己決定門檻（例如 p = .012 在 .05 顯著、在 .01 不顯著），也方便和別人溝通；臨界值法在考試和需要事先寫死決策規則時更清楚。
    A p-value lets readers apply their own threshold (p = .012 is significant at .05 but not .01) and communicates easily; critical values are clearer in exams or when a decision rule must be fixed in advance.
答案：(a) p = 0.01222 > .01，不拒絕；(b) 雙尾 p = 0.0244 < .05，拒絕；(c) 兩者等價，因為 z > z_α ⟺ p < α。Answer: (a) p = 0.01222 > .01, do not reject; (b) two-tailed p = 0.0244 < .05, reject; (c) equivalent because z > z_α ⟺ p < α.
Q8§09 · 拒絕域總表三種情況並排
題目　觀察值固定為 z = −1.90。對下面三種檢定，各寫出拒絕域、臨界值、p 值與判決：
(a) 雙尾 α = .05；(b) 右尾 α = .05；(c) 左尾 α = .01。
Question. Keep the observed value at z = −1.90. For each case write the rejection region, critical value, p-value and decision: (a) two-tailed α = .05; (b) upper-tailed α = .05; (c) lower-tailed α = .01.
顯示詳解 / Show solution
  1. 先把 p 值算出來 / p-values first
    左尾面積 Φ(−1.90) = 0.0287；右尾面積 1 − Φ(−1.90) = 0.9713；雙尾 2 × 0.0287 = 0.0574。同一個 z，三種尾巴配出三個完全不同的 p 值。
    Lower area Φ(−1.90) = 0.0287; upper area 1 − Φ(−1.90) = 0.9713; two-tailed 2 × 0.0287 = 0.0574. One z, three very different p-values depending on the tail.
  2. (a) 雙尾 α=.05 / Two-tailed
    拒絕域 |z| > 1.960。1.90 < 1.960，p = .0574 > .05 → 不拒絕。
    Reject when |z| > 1.960. Since 1.90 < 1.960 and p = .0574 > .05 → do not reject.
  3. (b) 右尾 α=.05 / Upper-tailed
    拒絕域 z > 1.645。−1.90 在完全相反的方向，p = 0.9713 → 不拒絕，而且離拒絕非常遠。
    Reject when z > 1.645. −1.90 is in the opposite direction, p = 0.9713 → do not reject, nowhere near the region.

  2. 樣本數不足的意義 / What under-powering means
    每組 1000 只有原本的 1/3，SE 變成約 √3 ≈ 1.7 倍大，power 會遠低於 80%。實驗就算「沒顯著」，也不能解讀成「新版沒有效果」——只是這把尺不夠利。這正是 §06 的「不顯著 ≠ 沒效果」。
    1000 per group is one third of the requirement, so SE grows about √3 ≈ 1.7 times and power falls well below 80%. Even a non-significant result cannot be read as "no effect"—ruler is not sharp enough. That is §06's "not significant ≠ no effect".
  3. 為什麼不能偷看 / Why no peeking
    每天檢定一次就是做很多次檢定，每次 5% 誤報。看 14 天至少一次假陽性的機率是 1 − 0.95¹⁴ ≈ 51%。而且「一顯著就停」會系統性挑到高估效果的那一刻。
    解法：事先算好 2943、事先定 α 與停止規則；若必須監控，改用序貫檢定或 alpha spending。
    Daily testing means many tests at 5% each; over 14 days the chance of at least one false positive is 1 − 0.95¹⁴ ≈ 51%. Stopping at the first significant result also picks an inflated effect.
    Fix: pre-specify 2943, α and the stopping rule; if monitoring is necessary, use sequential testing or alpha spending.
  4. 顯著還要看效果量 / Significance is not importance
    3 個百分點的提升是否值得改版，要看每個轉換的價值與改版成本。p 值回答「是不是 0」，不回答「夠不夠大」。
    Whether 3 points justifies the change depends on value per conversion and engineering cost. A p-value answers "is it zero?", not "is it big enough?".
答案：(a) 每組 2943 人；(b) 樣本不足 → power 低落，沒顯著也不能說沒效果；(c) 偷看 14 天假陽性率約 51%，應事先定樣本數與停止規則。Answer: (a) 2943 per group; (b) under-powered, so non-significance proves nothing; (c) 14 peeks give ~51% false-positive risk—fix sample size and stopping rule in advance.
Q18§18 · 實務陷阱診斷三種誤用
題目　下列三個做法各犯什麼錯？該怎麼修正？
(a) 每天看一次 A/B 數據，p < .05 就立刻上線。
(b) 同時比較 20 個指標，只報告其中 2 個顯著的，宣稱「全面改善」。
(c) 實驗結果 p = .0001，於是在報告裡寫「效果非常大，絕對值得全面上線」。
Question. What is wrong with each practice, and how should it be fixed?
(a) Check the A/B data daily and launch as soon as p < .05.
(b) Compare 20 metrics at once, report only the 2 significant ones and claim "broad improvement".
(c) The experiment gives p = .0001, so the report says "the effect is huge—definitely roll out everywhere".
顯示詳解 / Show solution
  1. (a) Peeking / Repeated testing
    每天檢定讓實際 α 遠大於 5%；「一顯著就停」還會高估效果。修正：事先算樣本數、一次看到底；或改用序貫設計。
    Daily testing makes the real α far above 5%, and early stopping inflates the effect estimate. Fix: pre-compute sample size and look once, or use a sequential design.
  2. (b) 多重比較 / Multiple comparisons
    做 20 次 α=.05 的檢定，即使全部都沒效果，至少一次假陽性的機率是 1 − 0.95²⁰ ≈ 64%。修正：事先指定一個主要指標（primary metric）；若真的要檢很多個，用 Bonferroni 把門檻改成 α/m = .05/20 = .0025，或控制 FDR。
    Twenty tests at α=.05 give a 1 − 0.95²⁰ ≈ 64% chance of at least one false positive even when nothing works. Fix: pre-specify one primary metric, or use Bonferroni (α/m = .05/20 = .0025) or FDR control.
  3. (c) 顯著 ≠ 很大 / Significant ≠ large
    p 值衡量的是「資料與 H₀ 的不一致程度」，不是效果大小。樣本很大時，極小的效果也能得到 p = .0001。修正：一定要報告效果量與信賴區間，例如「轉換率 +0.4 個百分點，95% CI [0.2, 0.6]」，再和成本比較。
    A p-value measures incompatibility with H₀, not effect size. With huge n even a tiny effect yields p = .0001. Fix: always report the effect and its CI, e.g. "+0.4 percentage points, 95% CI [0.2, 0.6]", then compare with cost.
  4. 三者共同的根源 / The common root
    三個錯誤都是讓資料主導規則：偷看是讓資料決定何時停，多重比較是讓資料決定看哪個指標，把 p 當效果量是讓資料替你做商業判斷。§15 的十步流程就是為了擋住這些。
    All three let the data dictate the rules: peeking lets data decide when to stop, multiple comparisons let data decide which metric counts, and treating p as effect size lets data make the business call. The §15 ten-step procedure exists to block exactly these.
答案：(a) peeking → 事先定樣本數；(b) 多重比較 → 主要指標或 Bonferroni α=.0025；(c) p 值不是效果量 → 必須報效果量與 CI。Answer: (a) peeking → pre-set n; (b) multiplicity → primary metric or Bonferroni α=.0025; (c) p ≠ effect size → always report effect and CI.

Q20§19 · 全課綜合從假設到決策
題目　某校全體平均考試分數為 70。老師想檢查新教法是否提高平均分。σ 未知。先做一個小型先導研究：n = 25、x̄ = 74、s = 10，α = .05。
(a) 寫 H₀、Ha 並指出尾巴。(b) 算 t 與判決。(c) 若正式實驗想在 δ = 5、σ = 10、power = .90、α = .05 右尾下進行，需要多少人？(d) 助理說「先導研究已經顯著了，正式實驗不用做」。這句話有兩個問題，是什麼？

(b) Compute t and decide. (c) For the full study with δ = 5, σ = 10, power = .90, α = .05 upper-tailed, what n is needed?
